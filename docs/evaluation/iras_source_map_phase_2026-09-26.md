# IRAS source-map audit (26 September 2026)

This audit uses the repository state before this phase as its baseline. The task brief supplies scope, not tax authority. Tax statements below were checked against first-party IRAS guidance or Singapore Statutes Online (SSO).

## Baseline and ownership

- The coverage registry already had corporate tax, GST, withholding tax, and employer topic labels. Many were classification labels without a canonical URL or explicit URL-verification provenance. `sourceStatus: VERIFIED` on a local tax record established neither URL reachability nor page-topic fit.
- Local tax knowledge already existed in `src/standards/statutes/iras.ts` and `src/standards/statutes/gst.ts`, including general deductions, prohibited deductions, capital allowances, loss relief, withholding tax, GST registration, reverse charge, blocked input tax, and historical GST rates. These records were retained; URL pointers are added alongside them.
- `src/standards/approvedSourceRegistry.ts` and `src/utils/statutoryLinkResolver.ts` already enforced approved hosts and URL provenance for rendered citations. `src/retrieval/controlledWebRetriever.ts` already validated final redirects, page titles, and topic content. The source-map phase uses these controls rather than a new tax retrieval system.

## Source-map boundaries

The catalog in `src/standards/coverageRegistry.ts` records directly checked page URLs, titles, topic relationships, verification date and method. `src/standards/unifiedSourceModel.ts` exposes those as topic-scoped `SOURCE_MAP_POINTER` records. A pointer contains no tax-rule text, is `NEEDS_REVIEW` for substantive content, and is not grounding evidence. A mapped page must still be fetched and pass final-host, title, and topic checks before an excerpt enters the current answer context. Fetched evidence stays `CANDIDATE`; discovery never promotes it into the static registry.

The topic links were narrowed during review: WHT filing/deemed-date guidance is not attached to the umbrella WHT scope/rates topic; AIS/IR8A reporting is not attached to directors’ fee timing; the GST input-tax guidance page is linked to the entertainment topic as a general route; and the foreign-tax-credit page is not presented as a map of all DTA rules. These are routing relationships only and do not establish topic-specific tax eligibility.

Existing validated local knowledge remains the primary answer path. IRAS live retrieval is a controlled fallback, not a replacement. Unmapped topics use bounded IRAS/SSO sitemap discovery. An unavailable endpoint, unrelated page, fabricated path, changed same-host pointer URL, or generic landing page fails closed. The final validated URL, rather than a model-proposed URL, is the only live citation candidate.

Historical questions require period-specific evidence. Current-only IRAS page maps do not establish a prior-year rule merely because a current page mentions that topic. Section 13W and Section 14N local summaries have explicit old/new YA periods; local GST rate records preserve their existing historical windows. Missing material facts are surfaced for specific deduction, GST, withholding-tax, and IR21 questions, including the service year that determines the corporate WHT rate.

## Remaining limits to track

- The map is broad but not a complete encoding of IRAS rules. A mapped official overview is a retrieval route, not proof for every subtopic or paragraph. Some niche topics remain discovery-only.
- Current HTML pages cannot establish every historical YA, temporary Budget measure, filing deadline, or old GST/WHT threshold. When no dated local rule or verified historical document applies, the answer must seek period-specific official evidence or state the gap.
- An explicit transaction date and an explicit tax YA in one question can refer to different years. The existing general target-date resolver prioritises the transaction date. Section 14N local summaries are excluded when those years differ, and the classifier asks for the company’s YA basis period; the answer remains conditional until that is established.
- The official taxable/non-taxable-income page links the 2025 fourth-edition Section 13W e-Tax Guide, but the guide is a PDF. Section 13W therefore remains discovery-only in this HTML source-map fallback until a PDF-aware path validates the guide; dated pre-2026 and 2026-onward local summaries remain conditional on disposal and shareholding facts.
- The offline fallback tests use injected official-page responses to validate the control flow. They do not prove that a user browser can fetch every IRAS page at runtime; site access and CORS may limit live retrieval.

The 43-case benchmark fixture records review status separately for routing and outcome dimensions. It includes pre-/post-2026 Section 13W, YA 2024/YA 2026 Section 14N, conditional third-party motor-car, WHT, GST meal, corporate deduction and return-eligibility cases, and dated 2023/2024 GST calculations. The retrieval reviewer independently endorsed routing expectations for 31 specified cases, including the carry-back topic with no current live source selection for unresolved relative YA intent and the WHT due/deemed-date query with only its dedicated topics and due-date map. The content reviewer independently endorsed selected substantive, missing-fact, effective-date and historical GST calculation expectations for eight cases. These per-case labels carry reviewer/date metadata, while every `answerPathOracle` and top-level `reviewedOracle` gate remains `PENDING_REVIEW`, so the benchmark does not score an end-to-end answer as approved. Root-level `reviewedDimensions` and `unverifiedDimensions` describe dimensions reviewed uniformly across all 43 cases; case-level review metadata controls partial routing and outcome scoring. A source-map hit alone is not a passing substantive tax answer.

The classifier regression also keeps employee tax-assessment timing questions and passenger-car tax-deductibility questions in the relevant IRAS domain when `director` or `depreciation` appears incidentally. Explicit accounting or journal analysis alongside tax still routes to both authorities.

The independent content review identified the GST, WHT, Section 14N, transition and missing-fact issues above; regression coverage was added for the material corrections. A separate retrieval/source review checked URL provenance, redirects, lifecycle and citation behavior and prompted narrower topic mappings and historical fallback guards.
