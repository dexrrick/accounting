The consumed targeted acceptance V3 is an authentic fail-closed **local capability probe**, but its combination of `localOnly` and mandatory complete VERIFIED IRAS rule evidence is structurally inconsistent as an acceptance contract for the normal production evidence architecture. Its failures do not establish a live retrieval regression.

The preregistration explicitly fixes `evidenceMode=localOnly`. `groundingContextBuilder.ts` makes the mapped fallback topic list empty and disables authority discovery in that mode. CPF relief, foreign income and residency have empty, non-grounding source-map pointers instead of reviewed local substantive evidence. Consequently their release gates cannot pass in this profile, independently of semantic quality. The V3 contract does not require or authorize adding substantive local rule text. General individual reliefs is routing-only. Admission of a local summary also does not establish complete literal rule coverage.

Production's intended path remains reviewed local evidence, then mapped official evidence for local topic/concept gaps, then governed discovery if necessary, followed by admission, literal claim verification and per-issue coverage. Audited V9 independently reached VERIFIED rule evidence for private expense, foreign income, residency, WHT and GST. It did not establish CPF relief live evidence or whole-question acceptance for every application.

For a new prospective profile, preregister separate contracts:

1. Semantic interpretation and topic/authority ownership: fixed case templates and semantic issue matching, complete inventory accounting, contextual authorities preserved, material omissions fail closed. Historical subjects cannot be inferred or rescored.
2. Local capability: expected missing local evidence remains INSUFFICIENT; complete VERIFIED lifecycle is required only where reviewed local substantive evidence genuinely supports every requested rule. Pointers never become evidence.
3. Rule evidence lifecycle: API-free controlled evidence fixtures through the real governed pipeline, preserving provenance, exact quotation, qualifications, topic association and requested-concept checks. Synthetic excerpts must be labelled synthetic; V9 hashes/counts are observations, not reconstructible source quotations. Any later live acceptance needs its own explicitly chosen evidence mode, source inventory, independent review, consumption reservation and fingerprints.
4. Application: case-specific relief, private expense and foreign dividend remain UNRESOLVED until facts/application are verified; general rules remain NOT_REQUIRED. Rule VERIFIED must not imply overall VERIFIED.
5. Negative controls: unsupported SFRS(I) 6 remains visible, NO_COVERAGE_TOPIC, without invented evidence or an IRAS route; wrong authority/domain, unrelated subjects, missing conditions and quote-only shortcuts must remain rejected.

Do not implement a new acceptance runner or consume another version until proven production ownership defects and prospective scoring controls are independently reviewed. No frozen V1–V9 or targeted V3 artifact is changed by this decision.
