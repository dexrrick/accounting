Financial statements: Filing requirements & exemptions
Last updated on 20 October 2026.

Who must prepare and file
All Singapore-incorporated companies must:
Prepare financial statements, except dormant relevant companies.
File financial statements with ACRA, unless exempted.
All financial statements that you file as part of your annual return will be available for public purchase.

Dormant relevant company
You do not need to prepare and file financial statements if your company meets all of the requirements under section 201A of the Companies Act.
Your company meets the substantial assets test. You need to meet either criterion to pass the substantial assets test:
Your total assets at any time during the financial year did not exceed $500,000.
As a parent company, the consolidated total assets of your group at any time during the financial year did not exceed $500,000.
Your company has been dormant from either the time of formation or the end of the previous financial year. A company is considered dormant when it has no significant accounting transactions during the financial year. An accounting transaction is one that affects the financial statements of a company.
Your company is not a listed company or a subsidiary of a listed company.

Solvent exempt private company (EPC)
You do not need to file financial statements if you meet both of the conditions below. However, you can choose to file financial statements voluntarily.
You need to meet either EPC requirement:
Private company: You have fewer than 20 members and no corporation holds beneficial interest in your shares (directly or indirectly).
Government-owned company: You are a private company owned by the Government and declared an EPC by Gazette.
You are solvent where you can meet your debts as and when they are due. Note: You need to make an online declaration of solvency when you file your annual return.

Filing requirements for companies
The type and extent of filing depend on your company's nature and the size of its operations.
Smaller and non-publicly accountable companies, including insolvent EPCs that cannot pay their debts as and when they are due:
Simplified XBRL financial statements; and a PDF copy of your financial statements authorised by directors.
Banks, finance and insurance companies:
XBRL FSH (Banks) or XBRL FSH (Insurance) financial statements; and a PDF copy of your financial statements authorised by directors.
Companies limited by guarantee; or companies using other accounting standards upon approval by ACRA:
PDF copy of your financial statements authorised by directors.
All other Singapore-incorporated companies, including insolvent EPCs that are not small and non-publicly accountable and are required to file financial statements:
Full XBRL financial statements.
Solvent EPCs which voluntarily choose to file:
(Optional) Financial statements in XBRL format. The required XBRL template (Full XBRL or Simplified XBRL) depends on the size of your company's operations and whether it is a non-publicly accountable company.
(Optional) PDF copy of your financial statements authorised by directors.

Smaller and non-publicly accountable companies
A smaller company is one where both conditions are met:
Revenue for the current financial year does not exceed $500,000.
Total assets for the current financial year do not exceed $500,000.
What is a non-publicly accountable company
Your company is non-publicly accountable if it is:
Not a listed company.
Not a banking and payment system entity.
Not an insurance entity.
Not a capital market infrastructure provider.
Not a capital markets intermediary.
Not any other regulated entity.

Types of XBRL templates
XBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language) is a digital format that captures financial data in a structured way.
Full XBRL template: About 210 data elements; primary statements and selected notes to the financial statement.
Simplified XBRL template: About 120 data elements; complete information in financial performance and position statements and selected notes to the financial statement.
XBRL FSH (banks) template: About 80 data elements for banking companies.
XBRL FSH (insurance) template: About 80 data elements for insurance companies.
