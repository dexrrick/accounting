## Changes

evidenceGroundedSynthesis.ts builds answer context from already admitted issue-level quotations, IRAS-policy results, source metadata, case facts, unresolved issues and deterministic outputs. It merges existing workstream and grounding-context evidence rather than replacing either source pipeline. A separate call through the same configured provider assesses each generated claim against its cited passages and facts. One targeted revision is permitted; supported content survives failed revisions. Source admission is recorded separately from model-assessed semantic support.

geminiService.ts runs the existing governed query pipeline first, captures its context/workstreams and conversation scope, then synthesizes eligible evidence-backed answers. An unmapped IRAS issue can reuse the existing scoped retrieval and evidence policy while retaining all original completeness gaps. Complete local output-GST calculators retain their network-free fast path. Scenario and journal state remain owned by the existing engine. Images and Phase 1 full-answer/failure paths retain their established handling.

aiFirstAdvisory.ts strengthens statutory-requirement and third-party applicability routing while preserving conceptual audience and conditional-explanation controls. App.tsx, ChatPanel.tsx, conversationBoundary.ts and accounting.ts expose safe failure causes and retry with the original question, scenario and conversation prefix. Unsupported numeric assertions, including Unicode numerals and mislabelled claims, cannot be authorized by a positive model-review verdict. Failed revisions preserve sanitized error categories whether a partial answer survives or the governed fallback is returned.

Tests: test_ai_first_advisory_phase1.mjs, test_evidence_grounded_synthesis.mjs and test_evidence_grounded_synthesis_pipeline.mjs; the new suites are registered in scripts/run_all_tests.mjs. No source-registry, statutory-content, dependency or deployment-configuration changes.
