Changes
1. src/services/semanticQuestionUnderstanding.ts preserves explicit IR8A, IR8S, AIS and employment-income reporting asks in mixed CPF/bonus questions. Verb-led wording such as "report the annual bonus as employment income" is covered. CPF-only bonus questions still suppress incidental IRAS topics.
2. src/retrieval/cpfPayrollKnowledge20261027b.ts collects all OW candidates and withholds a single monthly base when employee/month ownership is ambiguous. It does not introduce a general per-employee payroll parser. Existing single-employee OW ceiling and bonus/OW separation controls remain.
3. The same CPF module normalizes employee/staff/worker identifiers and question-owner case. One employee's PR grant date cannot determine another's personal boundaries or conversion-month OW proration. The owner detector selects the actual question rather than an appended issue subject.
4. src/retrieval/targetDateResolver.ts provides one clause-scoped predicate reused by source admission and date resolution. A 2026 grant can produce 2027/2028 contribution-year boundaries. Separately scoped future payroll periods, unsupported rates and out-of-range years remain excluded. The older PR-year fallback is also guarded for current-grant cases.
Regression coverage extends three existing registered suites: test_cpf_payroll_knowledge_20261027b.mjs, test_statutory_cpf_expansion_20261101.mjs and test_statutory_multipart_issue_refinements_20261030.mjs. No statutory rates or source fixtures changed.
