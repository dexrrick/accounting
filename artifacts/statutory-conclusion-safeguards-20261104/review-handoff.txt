## Changes

src/services/evidenceGroundedSynthesis.ts adds a scoped, provider-neutral support check against cited passages, facts, scope, dates and deterministic outputs. Generated claim labels cannot exempt false statutory conclusions, including source-free LIMITATION claims and AGC-published CPF fallback evidence. Exact full-source RULE equality is the narrow quotation exemption. Invalid, missing, duplicate or unknown verdicts and verifier failures withhold affected claims while retaining independently supported parts. Structured RULE/CONDITIONAL_APPLICATION/DEFINITIVE_APPLICATION verdicts replace the conditional-wording regex heuristic; unresolved status cannot admit a definitive application even with a SUPPORTED verdict. Metadata distinguishes model support from deterministic admission and counts actual calls.

The existing numeric boundary normalizes currency magnitudes and preserves currency/sign. It rejects incomplete tokens, digit backtracking, unsupported suffixes and Unicode variants before the optional support check. Synthetic allowance passages isolate parser tests; they are not new statutory rules.

Four existing suites changed: tests/regression/test_evidence_grounded_synthesis.mjs, test_evidence_grounded_synthesis_pipeline.mjs, test_synthesis_numeric_provenance.mjs and test_synthesis_practical_answers.mjs. No accounting-engine calculations, statutory rates, source registries, dependencies or deployment settings changed.
